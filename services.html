FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our overhead door services in Oklahoma City.

Does Scissortail Door work on both commercial and residential overhead doors?

Yes. We install, repair, and service both commercial overhead doors for warehouses and businesses, as well as residential garage doors for homeowners in the Oklahoma City area.

Do you install and repair garage door openers?

Yes. We install new openers, including smart and Wi-Fi-enabled models, and repair existing openers, springs, cables, tracks, sensors, and remotes.

What types of commercial doors does Scissortail Door offer?

We offer insulated sectional doors, high-speed doors, fire-rated doors, and heavy-duty commercial operators for warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and retail buildings.

Do you offer emergency garage door repair?

Yes. We offer emergency repair service for doors that won't open, close, or operate safely, in addition to scheduled preventative maintenance.

What areas does Scissortail Door serve?

Scissortail Door is based in Oklahoma City, OK and serves commercial and residential customers throughout the Oklahoma City metro area.
