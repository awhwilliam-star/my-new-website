Our Story

Decades of Experience, One Goal: Doors Done Right

Scissortail Door was founded to bring honest, high-quality overhead door sales, installation, and service to businesses and homeowners in our community. Named after the graceful scissortail flycatcher — a familiar sight across the region — our company reflects the same values of resilience, precision, and being a steady presence you can count on.

From small residential garage door repairs to large-scale commercial overhead door installations, our team brings the same attention to detail and commitment to safety on every job, no matter the size.

Today, Scissortail Door is proud to serve commercial property owners, contractors, and homeowners with a full range of overhead door products, openers, and ongoing service support.
